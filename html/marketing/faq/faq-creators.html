Creator FAQ

Common questions about becoming a creator on OnlyPaws.

What am I subscribing to as a creator?

The creator subscription gives you access to the OnlyPaws creator platform, allowing you to monetize your pet content through subscriptions.

Are there any content guidelines I should be aware of?

OnlyPaws is designed for respectful pet-related content. Content should prioritize animal well-being, safety, and responsible ownership.

Creators must publish original content they own or have rights to use.

What content can I post?

Photos and videos of pets, routines, training, grooming, playtime, educational tips, and creator updates.

What content is not allowed?

Animal abuse, harm, exploitation, unsafe behavior, illegal activities, harassment, or sexualized animal content.

How do I get paid?

Payments are handled securely through Stripe and collected through subscriptions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Dashboard → Subscription / Settings → Cancel subscription.

Will I get a refund?

Payments already made are non-refundable.

Does canceling delete my account?

No. It only stops future billing.
