Fan FAQ

Everything you need to know as a fan on OnlyPaws.

What is OnlyPaws?

OnlyPaws is a platform where fans support pet creators and access exclusive content.

How do subscriptions work?

Fans subscribe to creators and gain access to subscriber-only content. Billing is handled securely by Stripe.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Go to Dashboard → Subscription / Settings → Cancel subscription.

What happens after I cancel?

Your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period.

Will I get a refund?

Payments already made are non-refundable.

Is there a cancellation fee?

No. You can cancel anytime.

What kind of content will I see?

Photos, videos, and behind-the-scenes moments shared by creators.
